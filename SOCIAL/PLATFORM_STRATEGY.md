# PLATFORM STRATEGY

## Facebook
Prioritize Reels, relatable posts, photo stories and audience questions. Keep the first seconds visually clear and captions easy to understand.

## Instagram
Prioritize Reels, carousels/photos, Stories and concise captions. Keep the visual identity consistent.

## YouTube
Use Shorts for discovery and 8–15 minute story/travel videos for deeper content. Build recurring series.

## Cross-platform rule
The same core story may be adapted, but do not blindly duplicate every caption and edit. Adjust opening hook, aspect ratio, title and CTA for each platform.

## Global language
Primary: simple English.
Optional: English subtitles and selected Arabic subtitles for Gulf-targeted posts.
