# VIDEO MASTER PROMPT

Use this framework for every video request.

## Master prompt
Create a photorealistic vertical 9:16 social-media video featuring the locked main character from the approved reference image. Preserve the exact recognizable facial identity, core facial proportions, hairstyle, beard/stubble pattern, skin tone and body proportions. Do not replace or redesign the protagonist.

SCENE: [LOCATION]
ACTIVITY: [ACTIVITY]
STORY: [ONE-SENTENCE STORY]
HOOK: [0–2 SECOND HOOK]
ACTION: [DETAILED ACTION]
DIALOGUE: [NATURAL SIMPLE ENGLISH DIALOGUE IF NEEDED]
EMOTION: [EMOTION]
CAMERA: [SHOT TYPES / MOVEMENT]
LIGHTING: [LIGHTING]
ENVIRONMENT: [REALISTIC ENVIRONMENT DETAILS]
ENDING: [TWIST / PAYOFF]
ENGAGEMENT: [QUESTION OR CHOICE]

Use natural human movement, realistic hands, believable physics, realistic skin texture, authentic environmental sound, natural facial expressions and creator-style camera movement. Keep the scene visually premium but not artificial. No watermark, no random logos, no unwanted text, no face swap, no character replacement, no deformed hands, no duplicate protagonist.

## Default visual settings
9:16 vertical, high detail, realistic depth, natural color, cinematic but believable, social-media-ready framing.

## Dialogue rule
Use short, natural English. Avoid long speeches. Dialogue should sound conversational and globally understandable.

## Output format
1. Concept
2. Hook
3. Full generation prompt
4. Title
5. Caption
6. Hashtags
7. CTA
