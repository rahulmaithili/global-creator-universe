# SERIES BIBLE

## Recurring series
1. Rahul Goes Global — destination stories
2. What Would You Do? — audience-choice stories
3. One Day, One Challenge — daily challenge
4. Food I Didn't Expect — food discovery
5. Cruise Life — ship and ocean stories
6. From Airport to Adventure — travel continuity
7. Expectation vs Reality — comedy
8. The Next Destination — audience chooses location
9. 60 Seconds Outside My Comfort Zone — adventure
10. Everyday Life, Unexpected Ending — relatable mini stories

## Continuity
Episodes can share:
- wardrobe progression
- travel route
- recurring friend
- recurring animal
- previous episode reference
- audience-selected destination

Continuity should be deliberate; it is not duplication.
