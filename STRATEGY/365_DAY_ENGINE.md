# 365-DAY CONTENT ENGINE

## Daily minimum package
- 2 short videos
- 2 photos
- 3 story concepts
- 1 audience interaction
- 1 weekly long-form concept

## Daily rotation
Morning: lifestyle/coffee/gym/routine
Afternoon: food/travel/exploration
Evening: comedy/story/adventure
Night: interactive question or mini-story

## Weekly rotation
Monday: USA everyday life
Tuesday: travel/city
Wednesday: food
Thursday: Gulf lifestyle
Friday: adventure
Saturday: cruise/ship/luxury
Sunday: global relatable story

## Monthly rule
Every month introduce:
- 4 new cities
- 4 new food experiences
- 4 new adventure settings
- 2 new transport concepts
- 2 new recurring characters/animals only when needed
- 1 connected mini-series

The exact schedule can be changed without changing the character lock.
