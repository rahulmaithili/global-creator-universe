# MASTER CONTENT STRATEGY

## 1. Content pillars
1. Universal relatable comedy — 20%
2. Lifestyle — 15%
3. Travel and city life — 15%
4. Food — 10%
5. Adventure — 10%
6. Luxury / cruise / yacht / premium experiences — 10%
7. Interactive choice and audience decisions — 10%
8. Friendship / wholesome / emotional mini-stories — 5%
9. Trend adaptation and experiments — 5%

Percentages are a planning guide, not a rigid quota.

## 2. Video formula
0–2 sec: visual or spoken hook
2–8 sec: setup
8–20 sec: escalation/action
20–28 sec: twist/payoff
final seconds: comment-worthy choice or question

For longer videos, expand the same story architecture instead of adding filler.

## 3. Engagement engine
Rotate:
- A/B choice
- What would you do?
- Guess what happens next
- Where should I go next?
- Which food should I try?
- Would you try this?
- Comment the next destination
- Choose the next episode
- Rate the experience
- Spot the hidden detail

Do not use "like/share/follow" as the only CTA.

## 4. Unlimited topic engine
Combine one item from each column:

Location + activity + emotion + conflict + twist + camera style.

Locations:
USA, UK, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman, Europe, Asia, islands, mountains, deserts, beaches, cities, countryside, airports, hotels, restaurants, cruise ships, yachts, trains, highways.

Activities:
eating, cooking, shopping, driving, flying, hiking, swimming, skiing, camping, sightseeing, working, gym, coffee, nightlife, fishing, boating, exploring, attending an event.

Emotions:
curiosity, excitement, embarrassment, surprise, confidence, confusion, joy, frustration, suspense, relief.

Conflict/twist examples:
wrong order, unexpected bill, missed transport, weather change, lost item, surprise invitation, unusual food, animal interaction, unexpected friend, hidden location.

## 5. Daily production system
Suggested baseline:
- 2–4 short videos
- 1–3 original photos
- 3–5 story ideas
- 1 audience poll/question
- 1 longer-form concept each week

Actual publishing volume should match production quality and platform limits.

## 6. Country rotation
Mon: USA
Tue: UK
Wed: UAE
Thu: USA
Fri: Saudi Arabia
Sat: Qatar/Kuwait/Bahrain/Oman
Sun: global/travel mix

Use authentic visual cues and avoid treating all countries as culturally identical.

## 7. Trend engine
TREND -> understand format -> rebuild with the locked character -> create a new story -> publish original version.

Do not copy another creator's exact script, visuals or identity.

## 8. Long-term brand
The face is the brand. The character should appear in multiple environments so the audience associates the personality with the creator, not with one location.
