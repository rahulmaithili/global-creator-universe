# CHARACTER USAGE RULES

## Always preserve
Face identity, facial proportions, hair family, beard family, skin tone and body proportions.

## May change
Clothes, shoes, accessories, pose, expression, location, weather, lighting and camera.

## Prompt placement
Put the character-lock instruction near the beginning of every generation prompt.

## Reference-image rule
When the generator supports a reference image, use the approved reference image for identity consistency.

## Multiple people
Other characters may appear, but the protagonist must remain clearly identifiable and must not be duplicated.

## Real-person transparency
The persona is a creator character based on the user's approved image. Content should not falsely claim fabricated residence, education, employment or official identity.
