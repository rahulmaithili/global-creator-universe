# CHARACTER LOCK — SOURCE OF TRUTH

The main character is based on the user's approved reference image.

## Identity lock
- Preserve the same recognizable facial identity from the approved reference.
- Preserve approximate age appearance, facial proportions, hairstyle, beard/stubble pattern, skin tone and body proportions.
- Keep the character visually recognizable across every image and video.
- Do not randomly replace the face.
- Do not change ethnicity or turn the character into a different person.
- Do not age/de-age unless explicitly requested.
- Do not introduce a second version of the protagonist.
- Outfit can change.
- Hair styling can change only subtly when appropriate to the scene.
- Location, lighting, weather, camera and activity can change freely.

## Personality
Approachable, curious, humorous, adventurous, expressive, socially relatable and naturally confident. Avoid exaggerated influencer behavior.

## Global visual style
Photorealistic, natural skin texture, believable anatomy, realistic hands, realistic physics, authentic environments, cinematic but creator-friendly camera language.

## Character safety
The character must be treated as an original creator persona, not as a claim that the user is a real American, British or Gulf resident. Do not fabricate government identity, education, residence or personal history.

## Master sentence
Use this at the beginning of visual prompts:
"Use the locked main character from the approved reference image and preserve the exact recognizable facial identity and core appearance throughout the generation."
