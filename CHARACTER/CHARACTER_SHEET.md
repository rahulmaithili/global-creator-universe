# CHARACTER SHEET — APPROVED REFERENCE

## Identity
- Persona: fictional global creator persona based on the user's approved reference image.
- Role: creator / storyteller / lifestyle explorer.
- Brand principle: same recognizable person, different places, endless stories.

## Visual identity
- Male, late-20s to early-30s visual appearance.
- Short, neatly styled black hair with a natural side/swept shape.
- Dark brown eyes.
- Medium-brown even skin tone.
- Broad/oval face.
- Straight, medium-sized nose.
- Natural, well-shaped eyebrows.
- Trimmed full beard with a neat moustache.
- Average-to-solid natural build; realistic male proportions.
- Default expression: calm, approachable, observant.
- Reference outfit: light/white textured crew-neck sweater. This is a reference outfit, not a permanent outfit.

## Recognition priorities
1. Facial identity
2. Hairline and hairstyle family
3. Beard/moustache pattern
4. Face proportions
5. Eye shape and placement
6. Skin tone
7. Body proportions

## Flexible elements
Outfits, shoes, accessories, weather, location, camera angle, lighting, activity and mood may change.

## Brand personality
Approachable, curious, funny, adventurous, expressive, relatable, confident without arrogance.

## Content identity
Food + travel + lifestyle + adventure + comedy + mini stories + audience interaction.

## Visual quality
Photorealistic, believable anatomy, natural skin texture, realistic hands, authentic environments, cinematic but social-media friendly.

## Hard negatives
No face replacement, no random age change, no different protagonist, no duplicate protagonist, no distorted hands, no extra fingers, no plastic skin, no cartoon look unless explicitly requested, no watermark, no unwanted text, no fake celebrity resemblance.
